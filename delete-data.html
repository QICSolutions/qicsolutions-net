Privacy

Delete your data

How to request deletion of personal data related to QIC Solutions apps.

Our apps are designed to work without a QIC Solutions account. We do not maintain a traditional user profile for most app features. You can still ask us to delete personal data we hold about you.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@qicsolutions.net with the subject line Data deletion request.
  2. Include the app name (for example, Frac Calc), the email address you used if you contacted us before, and any other detail that helps us find your request history.
  3. We will confirm and delete personal data we control (such as support emails) within a reasonable time, unless we must keep something for legal or security reasons.

What we can and cannot delete

  • We can delete support emails and other personal information you sent us that we store.
  • Purchase records for Pro unlocks are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. Manage or request deletion of store account data through Apple or Google.
  • Advertising partners (for banner ads) may process device advertising identifiers under their own policies. You can reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes app data stored on your device.

More information

See our full Privacy Policy or email support@qicsolutions.net.
