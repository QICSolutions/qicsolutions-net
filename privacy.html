Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QIC Solutions LLC (“QIC Solutions,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with our mobile applications (the “Apps”), including Frac Calc, 7 Tells, QIC Craftsman, and any other apps we publish under QIC Solutions.

By downloading or using an App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Apps.

1. Who we are

QIC Solutions LLC operates the Apps and this website at qicsolutions.net. For privacy questions, contact us at support@qicsolutions.net.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use an App, we (or our service providers) may process:

  • Device and usage information. Such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash or diagnostic data, and general usage events needed to run and improve the Apps.
  • Advertising identifiers. On versions of an App that show ads, advertising partners may use device advertising IDs and related signals to deliver and measure banner ads.
  • Purchase status. If you unlock the Pro version, the App Store or Google Play tells the App whether the one-time unlock was purchased so we can remove ads and enable Pro features. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.
  • Information you send us. If you email support, we receive the content of that message and your email address so we can respond.

Our Apps are designed to work without creating a QIC Solutions account. We do not ask for your name, address, or phone number inside the Apps unless a specific feature clearly requires it and asks you first.

3. Advertising (banner ads)

Free versions of the Apps may display banner advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (for example, Google AdMob or similar partners). Those partners may collect or receive device information and advertising identifiers to show ads, measure performance, and fight fraud or abuse.

Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies. You can often limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, Limit Ad Tracking / App Tracking Transparency on Apple devices, or Google’s ad settings on Android).

4. Pro unlock

Some Apps offer an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock a “Pro” version. Pricing is shown in the App Store or Google Play listing for that App and may differ by product and region. Pro removes banner ads and may unlock additional features described in the App.

Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google under their terms and privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that the unlock was purchased so the App can apply Pro status on your device (and restore it when you reinstall, subject to store restore rules).

5. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Apps
  • Deliver and measure advertising in free versions
  • Process and restore Pro unlocks
  • Respond to support requests
  • Protect against abuse, fraud, and technical issues
  • Comply with law when required

We do not sell your personal information.

6. How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Apps (hosting, analytics, crash reporting, advertising)
  • App stores (Apple and Google) for distribution and purchases
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety

We do not share information with third parties for their own unrelated marketing.

7. Data retention

We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and keep ordinary business records. Device-side purchase status stays on your device (and with Apple or Google) according to store rules. Advertising partners retain data under their own policies.

8. Children’s privacy

The Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can stop ad personalization through your device or platform ad settings.
  • You can unlock Pro to remove banner ads where that option is offered.
  • You can uninstall an App at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Email support@qicsolutions.net to make a request.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International users

If you use the Apps from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our providers operate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use of the Apps after an update means you accept the revised policy.
