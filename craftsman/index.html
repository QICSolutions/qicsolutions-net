Learning Path

Safety first, then build your craft

Lessons 1–4 build your core foundation: safety, DC theory, AC theory, then conduit bending. Complete them in order before advancing.

  1. Safety First Lesson 1 — hazards, PPE, and safe work habits (required)
  2. DC Theory Lesson 2 — Ohm's Law, circuits, and power calculations
  3. AC Theory Lesson 3 — RMS, power factor, and single/three-phase
  4. Conduit Bending Lesson 4 — EMT bends, calculator, and YouTube tutorials
  5. Apprentice Foundations Tools, residential wiring, and code basics
  6. Journeyman Core Commercial systems, code, and load calculations
  7. Specialty Skills Motors, controls, low voltage, renewables

FAQ

Common questions

Where do I start?

Lessons are coming soon. When they open, follow the core path through Lessons 1–4: Safety, DC Theory, AC Theory, then Conduit Bending. Safety is required before all other coursework.

Is the training really free?

Yes. Every course on QIC Craftsman is free to access. We believe professional development shouldn't be a barrier to entering or advancing in the electrical trade.

Do courses count toward licensing or CE credits?

We're working with state boards to pursue accreditation where applicable. Course pages will clearly note any approved credit status as partnerships are finalized.

Who creates the content?

Our curriculum is developed and reviewed by licensed master electricians, inspectors, and industry educators with decades of combined field experience.

Can my company use this for onboarding?

Absolutely. Contractors and training coordinators are welcome to use QIC Craftsman materials for apprentice and journeyman development at no charge.

Is there a place to ask quick questions?

Yes. Email us at support@qicsolutions.net and we'll get back to you.
